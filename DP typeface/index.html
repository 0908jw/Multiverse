Latent is a variable typeface shaped by the timing of communication. Inspired by typing bubbles and the blinking cursor, it responds to how something is written rather than what is written. The form shifts to reveal hesitation, tension, and distance in mediated conversations. What do you want to express beyond the words you type? Based on Etude by Order Type Foundry.
